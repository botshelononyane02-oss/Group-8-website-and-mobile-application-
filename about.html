About our academy

Helping people understand and care for animals.

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 by Sarah Smith in Durban, South Africa. The academy aims to help learners develop knowledge and skills in responsible pet care.

Our course options cover canine obedience training, pet grooming, animal behaviour, pet business management and essential short courses for everyday pet care.

Our Mission

To encourage responsible animal care by providing accessible learning opportunities and useful pet-care knowledge.

Our Values

  • Responsible and compassionate animal care
  • Respect for animals and their wellbeing
  • Continuous learning and personal development
  • Practical knowledge for everyday situations
